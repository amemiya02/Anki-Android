# 21: One-tap switch to the old study screen

**What to build:** When a Card needs something only the old study screen can do, the owner can carry on studying it with one tap. The old study screen also stays reachable from settings.

**Blocked by:** 19 (Study screen: Card Frame and answering)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] When a Card needs the AnkiDroid JavaScript API, the warning offers to open the old study screen, and one tap does so on the same Card.
- [ ] The switch lasts for that study session; the default stays the newer study screen.
- [ ] A setting chooses the old study screen as the default.
- [ ] The old study screen looks and behaves as it does today.
- [ ] A rendered-screen test covers the warning and the switch.
