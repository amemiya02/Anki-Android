# 17: Deck list: Decks, counts and starting a session

**What to build:** The owner opens the app to a redesigned deck list, sees what is due in each Deck at a glance, and starts studying a Deck with one tap.

**Blocked by:** 15 (Bottom bar navigation on phones)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Decks show as a tree; sub-Decks expand and collapse, and the state is remembered.
- [ ] Each Deck shows its new, learning and due counts as the Mockup presents them.
- [ ] The total due today and today's study summary are visible.
- [ ] Tapping a Deck with Cards starts a study session; tapping a finished Deck opens the finished screen.
- [ ] With no Decks, the empty state tells a Newcomer what to do first.
- [ ] Long Deck names in Chinese and Japanese stay readable and never push the counts off screen.
- [ ] On a tablet-sized screen the surface still works in its existing layout.
- [ ] It matches the agreed Mockup.
- [ ] It passes the Quality Bar at the rendered-screen seam: Light, Dark and Black; English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and one right-to-left language; normal and 200% font size.
- [ ] Screen-logic tests and Engine tests still pass unchanged.
