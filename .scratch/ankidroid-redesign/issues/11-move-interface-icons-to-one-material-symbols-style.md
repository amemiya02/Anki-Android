# 11: Move interface icons to one Material Symbols style

**What to build:** Every icon in the interface comes from one family in one style and weight, tinted from the Foundation. Done in batches by area of the app.

**Blocked by:** 07 (Foundation: colours, shape and the three themes)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Every interface icon is a Material Symbols icon in the chosen style and weight.
- [ ] Icons take their colour from a Foundation colour role, not from a colour inside the icon.
- [ ] Icons that carry direction mirror in a right-to-left language.
- [ ] Every icon-only control keeps its accessibility label.
- [ ] No two controls that do different things share an icon.
