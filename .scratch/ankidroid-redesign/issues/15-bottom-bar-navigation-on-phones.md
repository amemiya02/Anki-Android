# 15: Bottom bar navigation on phones

**What to build:** On a phone, Decks, Browse, Stats and More are one tap away in a bottom bar, and the side drawer is gone. More leads to settings, help and support.

**Blocked by:** 14 (Mockups for the Core Loop)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The bottom bar shows Decks, Browse, Stats and More, and each tab opens its existing screen.
- [ ] More is a new surface listing settings, help and support, and each entry opens what the drawer's entry opened.
- [ ] The side drawer no longer exists on phones.
- [ ] The system back gesture returns to Decks from another tab, and leaves the app from Decks.
- [ ] On a tablet-sized screen the drawer and two-pane layouts still work.
- [ ] It matches the agreed Mockup.
- [ ] It passes the Quality Bar at the rendered-screen seam: Light, Dark and Black; English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and one right-to-left language; normal and 200% font size.
