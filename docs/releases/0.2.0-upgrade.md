## Installed upgrade

The upgrade walkthrough used a Pixel 9 emulator with Android 15 (API 35), ARM64, a display override of 720 × 1616 at 280 dpi and 60 Hz, English (US), 100% font scale, and system animations disabled. The study day was November 20, 2026. The fixture used local storage. [Walkthrough](../../tools/joint-release/README.md)

The historical ARM64 APK had SHA-256 `475a76c92e8b842d05912eb8102bdab69df389cf4235a002e75d4525964c31da`. The candidate APK had SHA-256 `132204962388e62cc3cb0bbb085042e2ce31467891566c5181b09346cbbe5e67`. The imported [shared collection](../../AnkiDroid/src/androidTest/assets/hayaki-release/collection.colpkg) had SHA-256 `63ba3eba14a180e13bcb50300009e0e9d2961e8cee93a193af40facf95bf1612`.

On the historical build, About displayed `2.26.0alpha14`. With **Follow system** and the **Black** night theme, the app used a black background when the device entered dark mode. In Controls, I bound **K** to **Show answer** and enabled **Hide ‘Hard’ and ‘Easy’ buttons** in Studying. I opened Geography, revealed a Card with **K** and rated it **Good**. The collection then held five Decks, eight Notes, nine Cards and five review records, including one written by the historical app during the walkthrough.

`adb install -r` returned `Success`. The package manager kept the original installation timestamp and reported `0.2.0` at version code `322600300`. After relaunch, About displayed `0.2.0`. Decks still showed nine Cards to study and one studied, with four new, three learning and two due. Read-only snapshots showed the same Decks and Notes, Card IDs and scheduling fields, and all five review records.

The candidate used a black Decks background in system dark mode and a light background in system light mode. In Geography, pressing **K** still revealed the Card, and the reviewer offered **Again** and **Good**. The saved night theme, keyboard binding and rating-button preference matched the historical snapshot.
