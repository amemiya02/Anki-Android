Installed version `0.2.0` had effective ARM64 version code `322600300`. The tested APK SHA-256 was `f5dda797e10d23c8d3aa0f935d36afbabeb0b112f1e3848f6e78b91a8b3d2ece`.

Both replays used the Android 16 API 36 Google APIs ARM64 emulator (`BE2A.250530.026.F3`, 60 Hz), TalkBack `16.0.0.738667889`, English (US), Hayaki Theme, Light Appearance, UTC, and 200% text. Window, transition, and animator scales were all zero. TalkBack stayed bound with touch exploration and double-tap handling active. The driver sent touches through the emulator's virtual touchscreen. The observer read Android accessibility focus, including the child labels of focused buttons, without suppressing the accessibility service. Audio is outside the recorded evidence.

## Reproduce

1. Install the signed ARM64 APK from the packaging run. Set font scale to 2.0 and turn off system animations. Use Light Appearance and set the device to the same UTC study day on both profiles.
2. Restore a fresh copy of the [shared collection](../../AnkiDroid/src/androidTest/assets/hayaki-release/README.md) through Decks, Import, Collection package. Use the same package for each profile. Its SHA-256 is `63ba3eba14a180e13bcb50300009e0e9d2961e8cee93a193af40facf95bf1612`.
3. Enable TalkBack with its default touch gestures. If Decks starts centred on Vocabulary, use a two-finger downward scroll to reach Today's summary. Explore the heading, then swipe right through the study count, New, Learning, Due, and Study All. The starting counts are 9 left, 0 studied, 5 new, 2 learning and 2 due.
4. Double-tap Study All. On Geography, move forward through the Deck title and the New 2, Learning 0, and Due 1 counts. Double-tap Show answer. On the phone, swipe right from Again through Hard, Good, and Easy, checking that each rating has its interval. Rate three Cards Easy.
5. On Finished, move forward through Reviewed 3, Time spent, and the four rating totals. Continue past Custom study and Deck options to Done, then double-tap it. On Languages › Spanish, swipe down then left to leave the review before rating a Card. Back on Decks, confirm 6 left, 3 studied, 3 new, 2 learning and 1 due.
