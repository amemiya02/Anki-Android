# Design System: Hayaki

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Standard, Played Straight"**

Hayaki is a stock Material 3 Android app, finished to the level of Google Calendar, Keep and Tasks. Its look is the category standard and nothing more: one fixed baseline scheme seeded from Baseline Violet, tonal surface containers that group the work, Material's type scale set in the system sans-serif, and the stock control a fluent Android user already trusts wherever one exists. Character comes from finish rather than costume: counts that line up exactly, a progress ring that moves on when a session ends, answer buttons that settle under the thumb.

The density serves someone who studies every day. Many Decks fit on one screen (56dp top-level rows, 48dp nested rows, 8dp between groups), counts never move when a Chinese or Japanese Deck name runs long, and on the study screen the Card Frame recedes so Card Content leads. Anki's own signal colours survive exactly where Anki uses them, on the new, learning and due counts and on the names of the four answers; everything else stays inside the scheme.

The system rejects the paper-and-serif costume of the earlier Quiet paper direction and AnkiDroid's flat table of numbers. It also refuses gamification, a generic template look, few Decks per screen, and motion that slows answering.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One fixed Material 3 scheme seeded from #6750A4, in Light, Dark and Black (true-black surfaces); never wallpaper colour.
- Tonal grouping: related rows share one surface container, and groups are separated by space, not lines.
- The system sans-serif (Roboto, with the system CJK font for the interface language); hierarchy by size and weight only.
- Stock Material 3 controls with the Foundation's shapes and type; custom drawing only for Anki's counts and answers.
- Flat at rest: only the add button casts a shadow.
- Short, functional motion; nothing between an answer and the next Card takes longer than 120ms.

## Colors

The Material 3 baseline palette, fixed: a violet primary, lavender and lilac containers, violet-tinged neutrals, with Anki's blue, red and green kept as signals.

Every colour is read by role, from `MaterialTheme.colorScheme` or `MaterialTheme.foundation.colors` in Compose and `?attr/colorX` or `?attr/foundationX` in XML. The values are generated from `docs/redesign/material-3.values.json`, and `FoundationTest` fails if a resource drifts from it. Keys ending in `-dark` are the Dark theme. Black takes every Dark value except the five `-black` keys: the surface family and the hairline.

### Primary
- **Baseline Violet** (#6750A4; Dark and Black #D0BCFF): the one filled action on a screen (Study, Show answer, Back to decks, a dialog's confirm), the summary ring and the study progress fill, a focused field's outline, text-button labels, and the emphasis that marks the current Deck and filtered Decks in the deck list. The Foundation's `primary`, `emphasis` and `progressFill` are all this role.
- **Lavender Container** (#EADDFF; Dark and Black #4F378B): today's summary card, the add button, and the round mark that heads the finished screen. Text and icons on it are On Lavender (#4F378B; Dark #EADDFF).

### Secondary
- **Lilac Tonal** (#E8DEF8; Dark and Black #4A4458): the tonal button, the navigation bar's active-destination pill, the actions the add button opens, a dialog's mark, the text-selection highlight and the study progress track. Content on it is #4A4458 (Dark #E8DEF8).

### Error
- **Error Red** (#B3261E; Dark and Black #F2B8B5): a destructive dialog action, a field's error outline and message, the study timer's limit. Its container (#F9DEDC; Dark #8C1D18) backs a warning dialog's mark.

### Neutral
- **Violet-White Surface** (#FEF7FF; Dark #141218; Black #000000): the window, the top bar, the study bar, the card frame's fill, and the ground under every group. The Foundation's `background` is this role.
- **Group Tint** (#F7F2FA; Dark #1D1B20; Black #0F0E12): Deck groups, list groups, and filled cards on XML screens.
- **Bar Tint** (#F3EDF7; Dark #211F26; Black #141218): the navigation bar and popup menus.
- **Raised Tint** (#ECE6F0; Dark #2B2930; Black #1D1B20): dialogs and the four answer buttons.
- **Ink** (#1D1B20; Dark and Black #E6E0E9): titles, Deck names and body text. The Foundation's `onBackground`.
- **Muted Ink** (#49454F; Dark and Black #CAC4D0): dates, captions, column headings, inactive navigation, answer intervals, zero counts, chevrons and the resting outline of a text field. The Foundation's `onBackgroundMuted`.
- **Outline** (#79747E; Dark and Black #938F99): the stroke of an outlined button on XML screens.
- **Hairline** (#CAC4D0; Dark #49454F; Black #3B3840): the 1dp stroke of the study screen's card frame and of dividers in dense tables. The Foundation's `outline` is this role.

### Anki Signals
- **New Blue** (#1F5BC4; Dark and Black #A8C7FA): the new count, and the name of Easy.
- **Learning Red** (#B3261E; Dark and Black #F2B8B5): the learning count, and the name of Again. It shares its value with Error Red but is its own role.
- **Due Green** (#1B6E3A; Dark and Black #8BD8A0): the due count, the name of Good, and the check beside a Deck that is done for today.
- **Hard Amber** (#7A5900; Dark and Black #F0C36D): the name of Hard.
- **Flags** (red, orange, green, blue, pink, turquoise and purple): Anki's seven Card flags, as marks.

The answer colours on Again, Hard, Good and Easy are recorded in ADR-0006 but have not yet been confirmed by the owner. Treat that line as provisional.

### Named Rules
**The Fixed Seed Rule.** One Material 3 scheme seeded from #6750A4, in Light, Dark and Black. Wallpaper dynamic colour is never used, and no surface writes a hex value; it reads a role.

**The Anki Signal Rule.** Blue, red, green and amber carry Anki's meanings: new, learning and due, the four answers, and flags. They never decorate. Accents and emphasis stay in the violet scheme.

**The Earned Pill Rule.** A count wears its colour and a tinted pill only when there is work to do. A zero is Muted Ink at weight 400 with no pill.

## Typography

**Interface Font:** the system sans-serif: Roboto for Latin text, with the system's CJK font chosen by the interface language, so Japanese gets Japanese glyph forms and Chinese gets Chinese ones. No interface font is bundled.
**Card Content:** whatever its Card Template sets. The interface never restyles it.

**Character:** Plain, neutral and multilingual. Material's scale, with each role named for its job, lets Chinese, Japanese and Latin text read as one family.

### Hierarchy
- **Screen title** (400, 32sp, 40sp line): the title of a top-level screen, such as Decks in the top bar (headline large).
- **Page title** (400, 28sp, 36sp line): the heading of the finished screen and of an empty screen (headline medium).
- **Dialog title** (400, 24sp, 32sp line): the title of a dialog (headline small).
- **Summary** (400, 22sp, 28sp line): the line that says what is due today (title large).
- **Deck name, Study title, Answer** (500, 16sp, 24sp line): a top-level Deck, the Deck path on the study bar, and Again, Hard, Good and Easy (title medium).
- **Row title, Lead, nested Deck name, Editor field** (400, 16sp, 24sp line): list rows, the explanation under a page title, sub-Decks, and editable fields (body large).
- **Body** (400, 14sp, 20sp line): ordinary interface text and dialog content (body medium).
- **Button, Count, Study count** (500, 14sp, 20sp line): button labels and counts; counts use tabular figures so their columns align (label large).
- **Caption** (400, 12sp, 16sp line): dates, secondary lines and the time-remaining estimate (body small).
- **Label** (500, 12sp, 16sp line): column headings, navigation labels and answer intervals (label medium). The current navigation destination's label is the same role at weight 700.

Read a role as `MaterialTheme.foundation.type.<role>` in Compose or `TextAppearance.Foundation.<Role>` in XML, and choose it by its job, not by its Material slot.

### Named Rules
**The Size-and-Weight Rule.** Hierarchy comes from size and weight only. No style is italic, set in capitals or letter-spaced, and every role sets letter-spacing to 0 in place of Material's default tracking, because Chinese and Japanese have no italics or capitals to fall back on.

**The System Face Rule.** Interface text uses the system sans-serif. Any font bundled later must carry the SIL Open Font License, and no interface font ever reaches Card Content.

## Layout

Phones are single-column with 16dp side margins, on a 4dp spacing rhythm (4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24 and 32dp). Every touch target is at least 48dp, including a Deck row's chevron and its block of counts.

- **Deck list.** The top bar carries the screen title with today's date beneath it in caption, and sync and overflow icon buttons. Today's summary card follows (4dp above, 16dp below). Column headings for New, Learning and Due stick to the top while Decks scroll; they appear only while some Deck has Cards left. Each top-level Deck and its open sub-Decks share one container; groups sit 8dp apart, sub-Decks indent 24dp per level, and rows are 56dp at the top level and 48dp nested. Counts sit in three columns at least 40dp wide, widened to the largest count so every column lines up. The add button sits at the bottom end, above the 80dp navigation bar.
- **Study screen.** The study bar (back, the Deck path over the remaining counts and time estimate, undo, overflow) sits above a 4dp linear progress inset by 16dp, then the card frame, then the answer area. The answer area keeps the answer buttons' height on both sides, so the Card never moves when the answer is shown; Show answer fills the slot the four answers will take.
- **Finished screen.** Back and the Deck path; then a 56dp mark, the page title, lead paragraphs 14dp apart, an optional tonal Unbury button, a list group of Custom study and Deck options, and a caption with today's study. A full-width Back to decks button anchors the bottom.
- **Long names and large text.** A Deck name wraps once, then ends in an ellipsis; the counts never move. The column headings shrink together to fit their column. Every surface holds at 200% system font size.
- **Right to left.** Layouts mirror, a closed chevron points along the reading direction, and the Deck path separator mirrors.
- **Tablets** (smallest width 600dp and up). There is no bottom bar; the navigation drawer and two-pane layouts remain, reskinned. A bespoke large-screen design is deferred.
- **Dialogs** are 280 to 560dp wide, held 24dp from the screen's sides.

### Named Rules
**The Group-Not-Hairline Rule.** Related rows share one surface container with rounded outer corners. Groups are set apart by 8dp of space, and rows inside a group have no dividers.

**The Still Counts Rule.** Counts hold their columns whatever the Deck name, language or font size. The name wraps or ends in an ellipsis first.

## Elevation & Depth

Tonal and flat at rest. Depth is container colour: the surface, then Group Tint for groups, Bar Tint for the navigation bar, Raised Tint for dialogs and answer buttons, and Lavender Container for today's summary. Elevation never tints a surface (the surface tint is the surface itself and the elevation overlay is off), so every container keeps its exact role colour in all three themes. Dialogs float over a black scrim at 32% opacity, without a shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Add button** (Material 3 level 3, 6dp, a neutral shadow at 30% opacity): the add-Note FAB on the deck list.
- **Add button actions** (Material 3 level 2, 3dp): the extended actions the add button opens (Get shared decks, Create filtered deck, Create a deck), shadowed as one family with it.

### Named Rules
**The One Shadow Rule.** The add button and the actions it opens are the only things that cast a shadow. Cards, groups, bars, dialogs and buttons are flat.

**The Card Frame Rule.** On the study screen the Card sits on the plain surface inside a flat frame with 12dp corners and a 1dp Hairline stroke. It is the one outlined container, so the Card Frame recedes and Card Content leads.

## Shapes

Material 3's corner scale (4, 8, 12, 16 and 28dp) plus named corners. Everything is softly rounded; nothing is square-cornered except the interior rows of a group.

- **Fully round:** count pills, the navigation indicator, icon buttons, the finished screen's mark and a dialog's mark.
- **28dp:** today's summary card and dialogs.
- **20dp:** every button: filled, tonal, text, destructive and the four answers. This is a full pill only at 40dp; at the 48 to 56dp heights Hayaki uses, a button reads as a deeply rounded rectangle.
- **16dp:** Deck groups, list groups and the add button. A group rounds only its outer corners; the rows inside are square.
- **12dp:** the study screen's card frame and filled cards on XML screens.
- **4dp:** outlined text fields.
- **Hairlines** are 1dp.

## Components

### Buttons
Stock Material 3 buttons with the Foundation's 20dp corner and the Button type role. All are flat.
- **Filled:** Baseline Violet with a white label. It is 56dp tall and full width for a screen's page action (Show answer, Back to decks, Get shared decks), and 48dp with 20dp side padding inside the summary card (Study · the current Deck). One filled button per screen.
- **Tonal:** Lilac Tonal with a dark lilac label, 48dp, full width, with a 20dp leading icon (Unbury and keep studying).
- **Text:** a Baseline Violet label on no fill, at least 64 by 48dp; a dialog's dismissive actions.
- **Destructive:** an Error Red fill with a white label and a leading warning icon. When a dialog offers one, its safe text action takes the initial focus.
- **Outlined** (XML screens): no fill, a 1dp Outline stroke.
- **States:** Material's ripple and state layers; disabled per Material 3.

### Answer Buttons
Four tonal pills in one row, 8dp apart, each in Raised Tint with 20dp corners and at least 56dp tall. The interval sits above the name in Label, in Muted Ink; the name is in the Answer role and its answer colour: Again in Learning Red, Hard in Hard Amber, Good in Due Green, Easy in New Blue. A press settles the button to 96% in 90ms under a ripple. When Hard and Easy are hidden, Again and Good share the width.

### Count Pills
New, learning and due counts in the Count role with tabular figures, centred in their columns. A count above zero sits on a fully round pill of its own colour at 10% opacity over the group (2dp by 8dp padding); a zero is Muted Ink at weight 400 with no pill. A Deck with nothing left replaces its three counts with an 18dp Due Green check and "Done today" in caption. The study bar shows the same counts, and only the queue the current Card comes from gets a tint, at 12%.

### Cards / Containers
- **Today's summary:** Lavender Container with 28dp corners and 20 by 18dp padding. A 64dp determinate ring (a 6dp stroke with round caps, Baseline Violet on a 16% On Lavender track) shows the percentage in Label, or a check when nothing is due. Beside it are the Summary line, the studied-so-far line in Body, and the compact filled Study button for the current Deck. Coming back from a session, the ring moves from where it last stood to the new value over 400ms with Material 3's emphasized decelerate easing.
- **Deck groups:** Group Tint with 16dp outer corners, no inner dividers, 8dp apart. A row is a 48dp chevron slot, the Deck name, then the counts. The 24dp chevron in Muted Ink turns 90° over 200ms as the Deck opens, and sub-Decks settle into place in the same 200ms. A 6dp Baseline Violet dot marks the current Deck when it has no sub-Decks.
- **List groups:** Group Tint with 16dp corners; 56dp rows with a 24dp leading icon, a Row title and a 20dp trailing chevron, in 16dp padding. They hold the finished screen's actions and the empty deck list's alternatives.
- **Card frame:** see Elevation & Depth.
- **Cards on XML screens:** Material 3 filled cards in Group Tint with 12dp corners, no stroke and no elevation.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** Material 3's outlined text field with 4dp corners, text at 16sp weight 400, and a Muted Ink outline at rest.
- **Focus:** the outline turns Baseline Violet.
- **Error:** the outline and the supporting message turn Error Red, and the dialog's confirm action is disabled.

### Navigation
- **Navigation bar (phones):** Bar Tint, 80dp tall, four destinations (Decks, Browse, Stats, More), each a 24dp icon over a Label. The current destination gets a 64 by 32dp Lilac Tonal pill that grows from the icon's centre over 200ms, with its icon in dark lilac and its label in Ink at weight 700; the others are Muted Ink. A press or focus ripples on the pill, never across the label. The bar hides as the deck list scrolls.
- **Top bar:** the surface itself, with no tint and no shadow. The screen title with the date beneath it in caption, and trailing icon buttons in Ink.
- **Study bar:** back, the Deck path in the Study title role with muted › separators, the counts and time estimate beneath, then undo and overflow. Under it a 4dp linear progress in Baseline Violet on Lilac Tonal glides forward over 300ms as Cards leave the session.

### Add Button
Material 3's FAB: Lavender Container, 16dp corners, 56dp, a 24dp add icon and a level 3 shadow. It opens its actions above it as extended buttons in Lilac Tonal (48dp, 16dp corners, an icon and a Button-role label, a level 2 shadow), and extends itself with its label while open.

### Dialogs
Raised Tint, 28dp corners, no outline and no shadow, with 24dp padding (16dp at the bottom), 280 to 560dp wide over a 32% black scrim, entering with the platform's short fade. An optional 44dp round mark in Lilac Tonal (the Error container when the dialog warns) leads, then the Dialog title, then Body content 12dp apart. Actions sit at the end, 8dp apart, and stack at the end when they do not fit in one row.

### Icons
Material Symbols Outlined at weight 300, unfilled, 24dp, one style throughout. Only the launcher icon is drawn specially.

### Named Rules
**The Stock Control Rule.** Where Material 3 has the control, use it (Button, FilledTonalButton, TextButton, OutlinedTextField, IconButton, the progress indicators) with the Foundation's shapes and type. Custom drawing is kept for what Anki has and Material does not: count pills and answer buttons.

**The 120ms Answer Rule.** Nothing between an answer and the next Card takes longer than 120ms, and an answer press settles in 90ms. With animations turned off in system settings, every motion is instant.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** read every colour by role (`MaterialTheme.colorScheme`, `MaterialTheme.foundation.colors`, `?attr/...`), so Light, Dark and Black all stay right.
- **Do** put related rows in one Group Tint container with 16dp outer corners, 8dp from the next group.
- **Do** give a screen one filled Baseline Violet button for its main action; other actions are tonal or text buttons.
- **Do** keep counts in fixed, aligned columns with tabular figures, and let a long name wrap once, then end in an ellipsis.
- **Do** check every surface in English, Simplified Chinese and Japanese; in Light, Dark and Black; right to left; and at 200% font size, with 48dp touch targets and WCAG AA text contrast.
- **Do** use Material 3's emphasized decelerate easing (cubic-bezier(0.05, 0.7, 0.1, 1)) for things coming to rest, and keep state changes near 200ms.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use wallpaper dynamic colour, add a fourth theme, or write a hex value into a surface.
- **Don't** let italics, capitals or letter-spacing carry meaning, and don't carry Material's default tracking onto labels.
- **Don't** bundle an interface typeface, and don't restyle Card Content.
- **Don't** divide the rows of a group with hairlines; space and container colour do that. Hairlines (1dp) are for dense tables and the card frame.
- **Don't** put shadows on cards, groups, bars, dialogs or buttons.
- **Don't** use Anki's blue, red, green or amber as decoration or accent.
- **Don't** add gamification: streaks, confetti, badges or celebration animation.
- **Don't** spend height on oversized Deck cards; the deck list shows many Decks per screen.
- **Don't** let any motion between an answer and the next Card run longer than 120ms.
- **Don't** bring back the Quiet paper costume: serif or handwritten interface faces, and lists ruled with hairlines instead of grouped.
